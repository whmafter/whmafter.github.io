Meet your Korean saju character

Saju is the Korean way of reading the Four Pillars of Destiny from your birth date and time. Your birth date gives you one of ten nature characters, and its face changes with each day's energy.

Every day

Yesterday, today and tomorrow: one key line, one thing to try, and a short reading for work, relationships, money and condition. A seven-day view shows how your character's mood moves.

Your lifetime chart

Your character, your eight signs, a personality summary and your five-element balance are free. The full report reads your chart as a whole in six parts (basic nature, your strengths and what weighs on you, relationships, work, money and your current ten-year cycle), with element balance and all ten luck periods.

Family and friends

Save the people you care about and see their characters and days. Check how two people get along with a score, its breakdown and a reading.

Shown, not hidden

Each reading shows how it was worked out: which part of the chart it comes from and what the saju terms mean. If a birth time or place is unknown, the app says what that changes.
