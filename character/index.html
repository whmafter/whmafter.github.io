Where your character comes from

Saju is the Korean way of reading the Four Pillars of Destiny, known in Chinese as Bazi: your birth year, month, day and hour each make a pillar of two signs. Your character comes from the day you were born. In saju, that day's sign stands for you. This sign is one of the ten Heavenly Stems (天干). Each day has one, and they come around in a ten-day cycle. The stem of your birth day decides your character.

Good to know

  • This is your character from the date alone. If you were born close to midnight, the app may give you the character of the day before or after, because it also uses your birth time and place (the local time zone and the sun's time there).
  • Your birth date is worked out in your browser and is not sent anywhere. The share link holds only the character. This page has no ads. Visits to this website are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies, and your birth date is not part of what it sends. GitHub Pages, which hosts it, receives the usual access logs (such as IP address and browser).
  • Your character stays the same. Its mood changes with each day's energy. It is one part of your saju, not your whole personality.

For entertainment, like the app. The readings follow traditional saju ideas. They are not predictions or advice.
