Only in the app

  • Every day: yesterday, tomorrow and the next seven days, the twelve two-hour periods, your week in words, a monthly fortune calendar, good-day finder and a daily log.
  • Your lifetime saju: the rest of the lifetime report (work, money, relationships, your life flow and more), the life graph, 150 question cards, the rest of the love report and a short primer.
  • Family and friends: save the people you care about to see their characters and days, full match readings, group matches for 3–7 people and invite codes.
  • Where you were born: add your birth city to adjust for the local sun time, plus a home screen widget and notifications.

Free in the app

  • Yesterday's, today's and tomorrow's readings
  • Character, personality summary, element chart, the report cover and your basic nature
  • Match score with its breakdown, what kind of pair you are, and today's match
  • Your 2027 flow, all five areas, and the months to use and to watch
  • The love report cover and how you are drawn to people and grow close
  • Daily log, saju type, your stars, a short primer
  • Saving family and friends, keeping readings you like, backup files
  • Home screen widget
