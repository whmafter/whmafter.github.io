Join the test

Saju Bomi is in a closed test on Google Play before it opens to everyone. Until then, its Play Store page opens only for testers. You can join the test and install the app in three steps.

1. Join the tester group

Open the Saju Bomi testers group on Google Groups and tap Join group. Use the Google account you use for the Play Store on your phone.

Open the tester group

2. Become a tester

With the same account, open the testing page and tap Become a tester. If the page says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

Open the testing page

3. Install the app

On the same testing page, tap the link to download Saju Bomi on Google Play. It can take a little while after you become a tester before you can install it.

Who can join

  • You have an Android phone.
  • You use the same Google account for the group and the Play Store.
  • You are 18 or older.
  • You live outside the EU and EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The test isn't offered there.

Good to know

  • Please stay a tester for 14 days. Google asks new apps to be tested for 14 days in a row before they open to everyone.
  • Purchases inside the app are test purchases for testers, so no money is charged.
  • The group's owner (the developer) can see each member's email address.
  • To leave, tap Leave the program on the testing page and leave the group.
